, a collection of 141 research techniques ranging from word clouds and node-link diagrams to timelines, maps, and matrix-based views. We also experiment with the [TensorFlow Embedding Projector](https://projector.tensorflow.org/)
, which shows how words can be represented as vectors and grouped by meaning. In Python, we practice turning text into vectors, creating text–matrix representations, and generating simple visualizations such as heatmaps and word clouds.
